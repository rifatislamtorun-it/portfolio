01 — ABOUT

Two disciplines, one way of thinking.

I started out troubleshooting switches and chasing packet loss across campus networks, and picked up web development along the way because I kept building internal tools to monitor the infrastructure I was managing. Now I do both, deliberately.

On the infrastructure side, I design and maintain routing, switching, and security for networks that can't afford downtime. On the development side, I build the dashboards, internal tools, and client-facing sites that sit on top of that infrastructure — with the same attention to reliability I'd give a production router.

I like problems that live at the boundary between the two: a slow app that's actually a DNS issue, a "network problem" that's actually a bad API call. Fluency in both layers is where I do my best work.
